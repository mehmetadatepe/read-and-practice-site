Privacy Policy

Read & Practice is an English learning application focused on reading levelled stories, listening, vocabulary review, and chapter practice.

Information We Collect

The app may collect account information such as an email address when a user creates an account.

The app may also store learning data such as reading progress, saved words and phrases, practice results, and app preferences.

How We Use Information

This information is used to provide account functionality, preserve learning progress, synchronize user data, and improve the app experience.

Third-Party Services

Read & Practice may use third-party services for authentication, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, translation, or AI-powered features. This policy will be updated as these services are enabled in production.

Data Deletion

Users will be able to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the support information provided on the Read & Practice support page.
